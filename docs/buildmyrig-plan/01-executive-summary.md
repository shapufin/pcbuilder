# 01 · Executive Summary

BuildMyRig is an online store where anyone can buy a ready-made gaming or creator PC, or assemble their own step-by-step with smart guidance that only shows parts that actually work together. Prices update live as parts are chosen, and the final build goes into a normal cart and Stripe checkout like any other purchase. The shop also sells individual components with powerful filters, so PC enthusiasts can buy just the parts they need. The team manages everything — products, prices, stock, and the compatibility rules — from a friendly admin panel with a visual rule editor, without needing a developer. Orders, payments, emails, and shipping flow automatically. The system is modular: the shop, the PC builder, and future features are separate reusable modules that can be enabled or extended independently.
